Made for the living room

One to four players

Everyone dances in front of the same camera, each with a colour, a score and a dancer to follow.

Your phone is the camera

No webcam by the TV? Scan the code on screen, prop your phone up and step back. It tracks you and sends the game your pose.

9:41

Tracked on your own device

Pose tracking runs on the device in front of you, and the game scores your pose, not your picture. A paired phone sends a small preview while you set up, which isn't stored.

Retro graphics

Every frame is drawn at 640 × 480, like a console from 2006, then scaled up to your screen with each pixel kept sharp.

Make your own routines

Dance Studio turns a recording of you, or a video you import, into a routine. Edit it, pick a stage and export a song anyone can play.

Experiments

Short games you play with your whole body. The first is table tennis: your hand is the paddle, against a robot or a friend.
