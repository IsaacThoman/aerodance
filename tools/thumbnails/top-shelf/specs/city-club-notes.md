# City and club concepts

All four specs use the game's existing stage, captured dancers, glass materials, lighting and post-processing. Cameras preserve the vertical composition at both 8:3 and 29:9; the wide version reveals more scenery horizontally. Logo positions use the renderer's 900-high design coordinates.

| Shot | Title | Composition and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `city-skygarden-panorama` | Skygarden panorama | A lower, nearer panoramic view than the existing city overview. The rooftop and pool form horizontal layers across the banner, with a gently enlarged duet at the lower center and the wordmark in the upper sky. The held wide-arm pose adds a clean silhouette without pushing subjects toward the edges. |
| `city-rooftop-duet` | Rooftop duet | A sunny character-led banner: the dancers move to the right of the terrace, while the title occupies the open left half. The near-level camera keeps both characters large and friendly, and the lower logo placement offers an alternative to a title running across the skyline. |
| `club-midnight-duet` | Midnight duet | An intimate near-level composition with the two dancers on the right and a large title on the left. The early club timing falls before laser activation, creating a calmer aquarium-and-neon mood distinct from the full floor spectacle. |
| `club-laser-panorama` | Laser panorama | An elevated diagonal view through the club with crowd, aquarium walls, stage and DJ booth spreading across the banner. The time lands just after a beat in a laser-active section; the top-centered title and larger spaced duet give the busy room clear anchors. |

The two character-led shots keep the dancers' feet around the lower quarter of the frame, leaving floor and reflections below them. The panorama shots keep their main duet close to the center, with the visual detail at either side providing extra width rather than essential content.
